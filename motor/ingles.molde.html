Parte expositiva 1

Quando usamos o Present Continuous?

Usamos o Present Continuous para falar de uma ação que está acontecendo agora.

SUBJECT + AM / IS / ARE + VERB + ING
I → AMI am studying.
HE / SHE → ISShe is reading.
YOU / WE / THEY → AREThey are playing.
She is reading. = Ela está lendo.
They are playing soccer. = Eles estão jogando futebol.
We are traveling in time. = Nós estamos viajando no tempo.

Negativas e perguntas

Forma negativa Coloque NOT depois de am/is/are.
He is not running.
He isn't running.
They aren't sleeping.
Forma interrogativa Coloque am/is/are no começo.
Is she dancing?
Are they studying?
Are you traveling?
Respostas curtas: Yes, she is. / No, she isn't.
